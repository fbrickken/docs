## Step 1: Choose Your enviroment
Welcome to the Brickken Token Suite.

• 👉 For **official tokenizations**, use the [Live DApp](https://dapp.brickken.com).

• 🧪 For **testing and sandbox purposess**, go to the [Demo Enviroment](https://dapp.sandbox.brickken.com).

> You'll be guided through the same flow in both environments.

![title](/images/step_1.png)

## Step 2: Create Your Account
If you already have an account, simply sign in.

To register a new account:

1. Click on **Create Account Tab**

2. Enter your email and password

3. Accept the terms and conditions

4. Click **Create Account Button**

> ⚠️ Make sure you use an email you can access at all times, you'll need it to verify your identity and manage your account securely.

![title](/images/step_2.gif)

## Step 3: Verify Your Email
You will receive a verification code via email. Check your inbox and copy the code into the verification screen.
> 📬 Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder or click “Resend Code.”

![title](/images/step_3.png)
