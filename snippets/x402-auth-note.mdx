Preparing charges the first half of the operation price, and `POST /send-transactions` charges the rest. Each paid call answers `402 Payment Required` with a `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header describing the chain, asset, transfer method, amount, recipient, and timeout. Sign each payment locally and retry with `X-PAYMENT`.

A valid `x-api-key` is accepted instead of paying, on both steps.

See [Authentication](/get-started/authentication) for the full credential matrix.
