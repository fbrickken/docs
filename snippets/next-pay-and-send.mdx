## Next step

Preparing is free and does not touch the chain. The response gives you `txId`, the unsigned `transactions`, and an `x402Requirements` quote of what the send will cost.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose who signs the blockchain transaction">
    In `client-signed` mode you sign the prepared transaction yourself. Where the operation supports `brickken-relayed` — the ERC-8004 and agent-token facades, plus the four signed RAMS lifecycle calls — Brickken's relayer signs and pays the native gas instead, and you sign only the payment. The other RAMS operations check `msg.sender` against an on-chain role, so they reject relaying; each page says which applies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit it and settle the payment">
    `POST` to [`/send-transactions`](/api-reference/endpoint/send). The first attempt answers `402 Payment Required`; read `PAYMENT-REQUIRED`, sign the payment locally, and retry with `X-PAYMENT`. An API key is accepted here only in `client-signed` mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll until it confirms">
    Call [`GET /get-transaction-status`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-transaction-status) with the `txId`. Relayed transactions need no credential to poll. Your payment settles only after the operation confirms, and is released if it reverts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Authentication](/get-started/authentication) for the payment handshake and [Pricing](/api-reference/endpoint/agentic-pricing) for the per-operation cost.
