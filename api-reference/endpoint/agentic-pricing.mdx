---
title: 'Pricing'
description: 'Mainnet x402 prices for Agentic API prepare and send calls.'
---

Prices are in USDC. The prepare charge applies to `/x402/...` facades and `POST /prepare-transactions`; the send charge applies to `POST /send-transactions`.

| Method | Prepare | Send | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `agentRegister` | `0.50` | `0.49` | `0.99` |
| `agentSetURI` | `0.25` | `0.24` | `0.49` |
| `agentSetMetadata` | `0.25` | `0.24` | `0.49` |
| `agentSetWallet` | `0.50` | `0.49` | `0.99` |
| `agentGiveFeedback` | `0.13` | `0.12` | `0.25` |
| `agentRevokeFeedback` | `0.13` | `0.12` | `0.25` |
| `agentAppendFeedbackResponse` | `0.25` | `0.24` | `0.49` |
| `agentCreateToken` | `5.00` | `4.99` | `9.99` |
| `agentMintToken` | `0.05` | `0.05` | `0.10` |
| `agentBurnToken` | `0.03` | `0.02` | `0.05` |
| `agentTransferToken` | `0.03` | `0.02` | `0.05` |
| `agentTransferFromToken` | `0.03` | `0.02` | `0.05` |
| `agentApproveToken` | `0.02` | `0.01` | `0.03` |

Sandbox and non-production environments use a reference price of approximately `0.01` USDC per call.

<Warning>
x402 payments and on-chain transactions are non-refundable. Confirm chain, signer, recipient, token address, and expected USDC price before signing.
</Warning>
