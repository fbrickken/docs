Prepares unsigned transactions for finalizing a Security Token Offering (STO). Use `POST /prepare-transactions` with `method=closeOffer`.

Finalizing is what unlocks [`claimTokens`](/api-reference/endpoint/prepare-claimTokens): until it runs, investors can neither receive their tokens nor be refunded.

## The end date has to have passed

<Warning>
  An offering cannot be closed early. The escrow refuses to finalize an offering that has not
  started, and refuses to finalize one whose `endDate` has not yet passed — returning
  `0xffac9d14` `IssuanceNotEnded(address,uint256)`. There is no administrative or time override,
  in Sandbox or in production.
</Warning>

An offering therefore runs for its full configured window. Since there is no minimum duration, give
test offerings a short `endDate` so you can exercise close and claim quickly.

## Success or rollback

What finalization does depends on whether the raise reached `minRaiseUSD`, the offering's soft cap:

| Outcome | Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Successful | Amount raised reached the soft cap | The raise is withdrawn to the issuer, and investors can claim their tokens |
| Rollback | Amount raised fell short of the soft cap | Investors are refunded their payment token instead of receiving tokens |

<Note>
  Waiting for the end date does not by itself produce a successful offering. An offering that ends
  under its soft cap finalizes into rollback, and `claimTokens` then refunds. To demonstrate a
  successful claim, size the soft cap so the offering can actually reach it.
</Note>

## Notes

- Only the issuer of the offering can close it.
- Existing investments remain valid throughout; nothing is lost by waiting for the window to end.
- Finalization is irreversible once the transaction is confirmed.
