## Next step

Preparing does not touch the chain. The response gives you `txId`, the unsigned `transactions`, and an `x402Requirements` quote.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose who broadcasts">
    `client-signed` (the default) — you sign the transaction and Brickken broadcasts it. `client-broadcast` — you sign and broadcast it yourself, then confirm with `txHash`. Either way the operation price is split evenly: half at prepare, half at send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit it and settle the payment">
    `POST` to [`/send-transactions`](/api-reference/endpoint/send). The first attempt answers `402 Payment Required`; read `PAYMENT-REQUIRED`, sign the payment locally, and retry with `X-PAYMENT`. An `x-api-key` is accepted here instead of paying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll until it confirms">
    Call [`GET /get-transaction-status`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-transaction-status) with the `txId`. Your payment settles only after the operation confirms, and is released if it reverts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Authentication](/get-started/authentication) for the payment handshake and [Pricing](/api-reference/endpoint/agentic-pricing) for the per-operation cost.
