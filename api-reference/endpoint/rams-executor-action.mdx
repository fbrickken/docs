---
title: 'Get RAMS Executor Action'
openapi: 'GET /rams/executor-action'
description: 'Read selector support and amount extraction settings from an AgentExecutor.'
---

Requires `x-api-key`. Provide `chainId` and either a 4-byte `selector` or left-aligned bytes32 `action`; optionally override `executorAddress`.

The response includes `supported`, `hasAmount`, and `amountIndex`, plus the executor's principal, owner, and AgentMandate binding. Verify value-bearing selectors have amount extraction enabled before relying on RAMS caps.
