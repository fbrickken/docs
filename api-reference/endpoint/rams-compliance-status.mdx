---
title: 'Check RAMS Compliance'
openapi: 'GET /rams/compliance-status'
description: 'Read a principal eligibility record from a ComplianceProvider.'
---

Requires `x-api-key`. Provide `chainId`, `principal`, and the bytes32 `identityRef`; optionally override `complianceProviderAddress`.

The response reports eligibility, expiry, and revocation reason details. A mandate can exist while compliance is ineligible, but `canExecute` will reject it.
