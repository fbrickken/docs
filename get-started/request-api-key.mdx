Brickken issues API keys by hand. Submit the form below and it reaches the team directly — no email to send yourself. It asks for everything we need, so the first reply you get is your key rather than a list of follow-up questions.

## What a key gets you

- The full **Dapp API**: tokenization, STOs, security-token operations, and reads — over HTTP or through the [MCP server](/mcp/introduction).
- The **Agentic API** in `client-signed` mode, without paying x402 per call.
- A plan with a **separate credit balance for each write method**, and higher rate limits on `prepare-transactions`.

Sandbox and production keys are separate. See [Authentication](/get-started/authentication) for what each credential reaches.

## What Brickken needs from you

The form asks for all of it. If you would rather email `tech@brickken.com` yourself, include:

- Your name, company or project, and a reply-to address
- The **email registered on your Brickken account** — it can be the same as or different from your reply-to address; [sign up for a sandbox account](https://dapp.sandbox.brickken.com/en/register) if needed
- Sandbox (API key requests are not currently available for production)
- Your target sandbox networks — Sepolia, Polygon Amoy, or Base Sepolia — and the token symbols you plan to issue
- What you are building, roughly which methods you expect to call, and your expected volume

## What happens next

A Brickken engineer replies to the address you gave, from `tech@brickken.com`. Whitelisting the wallet you will use as `signerAddress` is handled in the same thread, once we know which environment you are on.

Going to production? [Book an onboarding call](https://meetings.hubspot.com/ludovico-rossi/t1) so the right plan and limits are in place before you launch.

<Warning>
Never include a private key or seed phrase in this request. Brickken never asks for one.
</Warning>
