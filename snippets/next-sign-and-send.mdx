## Next step

Preparing does not touch the chain. The response gives you `txId` and an array of unsigned `transactions` — you still have to sign and submit them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign every returned transaction">
    Sign each entry locally with the wallet matching `signerAddress`. It must be whitelisted by Brickken, and it needs native gas on the target chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the signed payloads">
    `POST` them to [`/send-transactions`](/api-reference/endpoint/send) as `{ txId, signedTransactions }`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll until it confirms">
    Call [`GET /get-transaction-status`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-transaction-status) with the `txId`. A `pending` status means it is broadcast but not yet mined — do not resubmit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Full walkthrough: [Tokenize an asset and run an STO](/api-reference/guides/tokenize-and-run-an-sto).
