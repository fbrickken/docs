## Step 1: Welcome to the Digital Asset Store
You're now entering the Token Store, the space where you can:

- Discover tokenized investment opportunities

- Learn more about each project through documentation and terms

- Track and manage your holdings

> Take your time to review the information shared by the asset issuer.

![title](/images/step_2_invest.png)

## Step 2: Create Your Account
To proceed with the investment process, you'll need an active Brickken account.

1. Click on "Sign Up" at the top right

2. Enter a valid email address and password

3. Accept the Terms & Conditions

4. Click "Create Account"

> 🔐 Important: Use an email you can access regularly. You’ll need it to receive your verification code and stay informed about your investments.

![title](/images/step_1_invest.png)

## Step 3: Verify Your Email
Once you've submitted your account details, a verification code will be sent to your email.

1. Check your inbox (and spam folder just in case)

2. Enter the 6-digit code in the verification screen

3. Once verified, you’ll be redirected to the Token Store

> 📬 Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder or click “Resend Code.”

![title](/images/step_3.png)
